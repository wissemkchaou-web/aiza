Four concepts.
Four identities.
One program behind them.

Every Aiza concept has its own world, from Saya’s pink, floral rooms to the beach at Eva. Here are uniform ideas for Saya, Eva, Patriki and Mongol, front of house and kitchen, and how a single program can run all of them across Dubai, Almaty and Bishkek.

01 The ideas

A look for every concept.

Pick a concept, then switch between front of house and kitchen. Each look starts from the concept’s own character, so guests feel the brand before they open the menu.

These are starting points drawn from each concept’s character, with colours as our reading of each brand. Final colours, fabrics and artwork follow each brand’s own guidelines and are developed together with your team.

02 One program

Four brands. One way of running them.

Each concept keeps its own look. Underneath, everything that makes uniforms expensive to manage is shared, so a new concept or a new branch plugs straight in.

Unique to each concept

The brand layer

  • ColoursFrom each brand’s palette
  • Logo and artworkEmbroidery, print or woven label
  • Signature detailsPiping, trims, buttons, straps
  • Garment choiceShirt, polo, bistro or bib apron
Shared across Aiza

The program layer

  • Size charts and fitsOne standard for all concepts
  • Fabrics and suppliersFewer fabrics, better prices
  • Ordering and stockOne process, every branch
  • Tracking per employeeWho holds what, in every country
03 Built for working kitchens

Designed for service, not only for photos.

Open kitchens, beach terraces and busy brunch services put real demands on what chefs and servers wear. Every piece is tested on staff before it goes into production.

Heat

Fabrics that breathe

Lighter, breathable blends for open kitchens, beach service at Eva and Dubai summers.

Stains

Colour that forgives

Darker aprons and stain-resistant finishes where sauce, coffee and grill smoke are part of the job.

Brigade

Role you can read

Name and role on every jacket, with subtle differences for head chef, sous chef and commis.

Wear

Replaced on time

Each piece has a set replacement cycle, so tired uniforms never reach the dining room.

04 Three cities

Dubai, Almaty, Bishkek. One view.

Uniforms delivered to each branch, stock planned for new openings, and software that shows what every employee holds, per concept and per city. The software is optional and can be taken on its own.
